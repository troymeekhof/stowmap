Privacy

Stowmap reads your drives to measure them. What it finds stays on your Mac.

The app

  • Scans, results and the saved index of each drive are stored on your Mac only.
  • There is no account and no telemetry.
  • Stowmap checks for new versions so it can update itself. That check does not include anything about your files.
  • Items you collect are moved to the Trash on your Mac. Stowmap does not upload or copy them anywhere.

This website

  • This site has no analytics and sets no cookies. It loads no third-party scripts or fonts.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and downloads come from GitHub Releases. GitHub may log your IP address when you load a page or download a file, as described in GitHub's privacy statement.
